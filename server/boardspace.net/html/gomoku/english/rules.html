The Rules for Gomoku


 
SET UP

Start with an empty Go board, traditionally 19x19 intersection

First Turn:

Boardspace uses the "Swap2" opening rules.  The first player places 2 black and 1 white stone, anywhere on the board. The second player can then either:  
  • Choose to play white, and place their first white stone.
  • Choose to play black, and pass to the other player to play white.
  • Play 1 more white and 1 more black stone, and let the other player choose which color to play.
Subsequent Turns:

Players alternate placing one stone of their color on any empty
intersection.

Winning conditions:

The first player to achieve 5 in a row, exactly 5 not 6 or more,
wins the game.    

Additional rules for the "Capture 2" variant.  

In play:
    pairs of opponent stones "sandwiched" between to of yours are captured.
Winning condition:
    capturing 10 opponent stones is also a win.
