Gomoku

Gomoku is among the simplest possible games.   The goal is simply to make 5 stones in a row, playing one stone at a time, except for a small opening riff to blance the game.  Read the complete rules here.

Boardspace also hosts a variation called  Gomoku+Capture2 which knowlegable players will recognise as Pente.  Pente isn't a public domain name even though its commercial life seems to be over.

Robots:  Not to shabby at the tactics, but probably weak on strategy.
